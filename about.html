About FaceGrabs

Garmin watch faces made with a smile, for round AMOLED and color-screen watches.

Why we make watch faces

A watch face is the thing you look at most, so it should be your biggest fan, not a critic.

Each FaceGrabs face starts with a problem a watch face should solve. Most activity rings call every rest day a failure, most readiness scores are a number you cannot see into, and most faces look like another screen. We would rather build something that meets you where you are. We are all on the same journey, just at different stages.

The faces

  • Steady: Your journey isn't a straight line.
  • Output: Know when to push. Know when to rest.
  • FORGED: The watch your friends will notice before they even say hi.
  • Slab: Your watch, your brand.
  • Pea-k Performance: a free little green pea who grows with your week, on Connect IQ.

How we build

FaceGrabs builds from what wearers ask for, in five steps: idea, research, design, build, deploy. See how we build. Ideas we are exploring are on the In development page, and you can suggest a feature or say hi on the message board any time.
