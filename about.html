About FaceGrabs

FaceGrabs makes faces for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. Each one starts with a problem a watch face should solve.

Why Steady

Most activity rings call every rest day a failure. Steady measures the week instead of the worst day, so rest days count as part of the plan.

Why FORGED

FORGED is a watch face that looks like a piece of equipment, not another digital screen. Some things look better after they've been through a little hell.

Output and Slab Pro

Output is for people who train differently from the average, so the readiness score uses the metrics you choose. Slab Pro is for people who want big, clean time set up their own way.

In development

A companion Output widget is planned. It shows today's readiness score at a glance from the Garmin widget glance on the watch. No date yet.
