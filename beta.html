FaceGrabs Beta Lab

100 founding spots. By application only.

Your Garmin is personal. The way it looks, the information it shows and the way it fits into your day should be, too.

We are building a worldwide community of Garmin users who want to help shape what we make next. Not a bug-report job. Not a review club. A group of people who enjoy being part of making something better.

You wear it. You use it. You help shape it.

The Beta Lab is limited to 100 founding members. We hand-pick each group for a real mix of watches, sports, countries and experience, so every member counts. Founding members keep their spot and their founding status as we grow.

Why join

  • Get in earlyTry new watch faces, apps and features before everyone else.
  • Have a voiceHelp decide designs, features and where our products go next.
  • Get direct accessTalk directly with the people designing and building FaceGrabs.
  • Be a founderOne of 100 founding members of a worldwide community of Garmin fans.
  • Get recognizedCredit when your idea ships, special access, and partner perks as we grow.

You might shape a feature, catch a problem before release, or steer a brand new product. You are not just downloading something. You are helping make it.

What we ask

  • Try early builds and wear them in normal daily life.
  • Tell us what works and what does not: bugs, display issues, readability, design.
  • Suggest ideas and vote on what we build.
  • Join a testing round when it suits you. No obligation to test everything.
  • Once a product is public, leave an honest rating in the Garmin store if you like.

Keep private info private. We would rather you sign up with a testing email than your personal one, and we only ask for what we need: an email, your watch, and anything else you choose to share.

No programming or athletic background needed. We want honest feedback, not positive reviews. Perks and access never depend on whether or how you rate anything.

What happens to your feedback

You suggest it. We discuss it. We tell you whether we are building it and why. You see it built and tested first. When it ships, you get the credit.

Apply for a founding spot

Spots are limited and we review every application. All kinds of Garmin users welcome: everyday wearers, runners, cyclists, hikers, gym-goers, watch face fans and tinkerers, anywhere in the world.

We prefer a separate testing email over your personal one. Keep private info private. A spare address you check now and then is perfect.
