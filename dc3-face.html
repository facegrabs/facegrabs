3 of 4 · Watch face, $4.99

You decide what every spot does.

DC3. A silver and copper data face for round AMOLED Garmin watches. Pick what each spot shows, then hold a metric to look into it.

Get DC3 on Connect IQSee what is coming

What it does

  • Three ways to show a spot. A number, a name, or one headline for a group of metrics with a status dot for each.
  • Hold a metric for a full page with a titled graph and the numbers behind it. The face returns on its own.
  • Weather as a report: current conditions, 12-hour rain chances, a 3-day outlook, sunrise and sunset.
  • A battery page that charts the last 48 hours. It fills in after you install.
  • FaceGrabs metrics, worked out on the watch from your own history. Some need a few days of history first.
  • Your own groups. Recovery, Training and Today come ready, and you can build more with 3 to 6 metrics each.
  • Two looks, six accent colors and a dim always-on screen.

Coming in an update

Not in the store version yet

A face rewired by the hour.

  • A dark hex look with nine fields you choose.
  • Preset buttons: hold one to switch the whole face to that activity.
  • Day phases: the fields change for morning, day, evening and night, or stay in one fixed layout.
  • Web control (optional): pick the fields on a web page, then sync from the watch.
  • A daily note (optional, off until you turn it on): one short line each evening, written by AI from your step numbers.

Also in development

  • Nightly AI analysis and feedback.
  • iOS and Android apps for setting up DC3.
  • A free companion widget with graphs and trends over days and weeks.

Get it

DC3 is $4.99 on Connect IQ, for round AMOLED Garmin watches. The store listing shows whether your watch is on the list.
