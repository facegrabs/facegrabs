FORGED setup guide

Nothing to configure. Here is how to install it and read it.

What is FORGED and how do I install it?

FORGED is an analog Garmin watch face in weathered steel and rust, with a warm orange glow. It is built to look like equipment.

It shows the time with hour, minute and second hands, plus your steps, heart rate, battery and the date.

How do I install FORGED?

Open the FORGED page on the Connect IQ store on your phone, or search for FORGED in the Garmin Connect IQ app, and install it. It reaches your watch the next time the watch syncs. If it does not show up as your watch face, pick it from the watch face list on your watch. Want to wear it first? The trial listing runs the full face for two days.

Does FORGED have any settings?

No. FORGED has nothing to set up, on the phone or on the watch. Install it and it is ready.

If you want a face you can recolor and rearrange, look at Slab Pro.

How do I read the FORGED face?

  • The hands show hours, minutes and seconds. While the watch is awake the second hand sweeps smoothly.
  • Left sub-dial: your steps today.
  • Right sub-dial: your heart rate.
  • Bottom sub-dial: watch battery, as a percent.
  • The date plate: the day of the week and the day of the month.

How do I see a number that a hand is covering?

Touch and hold the watch face. The hands step aside for five seconds so nothing covers your steps, heart rate, battery or date. Touch and hold again to bring them straight back.

What does the always-on screen show?

On AMOLED watches the numerals and the hour and minute hands stay bright and the rest of the dial drops far back into the dark. There is no second hand while the screen rests. The view moves a couple of pixels each minute so nothing stays in one place.

Does FORGED look the same on every watch?

AMOLED watches get the full artwork with shadows and glow. Color-screen watches that are not AMOLED get a flatter version of the same dial, made for their 64 colors, and their screen stays on.

Why is FORGED not doing what I expect?

Why does a sub-dial show two dashes?

The watch has no reading for it yet. This can happen with steps or heart rate.

Why did the second hand disappear?

The watch has gone to its resting screen. The second hand comes back when the watch wakes.

Why did the hands vanish?

A touch and hold moves them out of the way for five seconds. They come back by themselves, or touch and hold again.

Why can I not find FORGED settings?

There are none. FORGED is one finished design.

Why does the face only show the time and "trial ended"?

That is the trial listing after its two days. Install the full FORGED face from the Connect IQ store to carry on.

Common questions

How do I change the settings on FORGED?

FORGED has no settings. It is one finished design, so there is nothing to set up on the phone or the watch.

What do the three small dials on FORGED show?

The left one shows your steps, the right one shows your heart rate and the bottom one shows the watch battery as a percent. The date plate shows the day of the week and the day of the month.

How do I hide the hands on FORGED?

Touch and hold the watch face. The hands step aside for five seconds so you can read everything under them. Touch and hold again to bring them back sooner.

Does FORGED have an always-on view?

Yes. The numerals and the hour and minute hands stay bright and the rest of the dial goes dark. The second hand returns when the watch wakes.

Which watches does FORGED work on?

Round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches including fenix 7, 8 and 9, fenix E, epix 2, many Forerunner models, Venu 3 and 4, vivoactive 5 and 6, Enduro 3 and 4, MARQ 2 and 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Approach S70 and Instinct 3 AMOLED. The full list is on the FORGED page and the store page.

Can I try FORGED before I buy it?

Yes. The trial listing on the Connect IQ store runs the full face for two days. After that it shows only the time and a note that the trial ended. The full face is $1.99 on the Connect IQ store.
