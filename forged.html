FORGED

Built to look earned.

The problem

Most watch faces look like a screen. FORGED looks like a tool that has been used.

Some things look better after they've been through a little hell.

What it does

  • Weathered steel, rust, scratches and deep mechanical detail under a warm orange glow.
  • A mountain valley with a small hidden detail in the terrain.
  • Three sub-dials for steps, heart rate and battery, plus a date plate.
  • The hands cast real shadows, and the second hand sweeps smoothly instead of ticking.
  • Has an always-on view.
