FORGED

The watch your friends will notice before they even say hi.

Built to look earned. Weathered steel, rust and a warm orange glow that looks like equipment, not another digital screen.

Get FORGED on Connect IQTry it for two days

A watch that says something about you

Most watch faces look like a screen. FORGED looks like a tool that has been used, and it says something about you before you say a word.

Some things look better after they have been through a little hell. FORGED is built on that idea: weathered steel, rust and scratches, lit by a warm orange glow, so your wrist carries something that looks like it earned its place.

Every scratch earned

  • Deep mechanical detail. Weathered steel, rust and scratches under a warm orange glow.
  • A mountain valley behind the dial, with a small hidden detail in the terrain for you to find.
  • Three sub-dials for steps, heart rate and battery, plus a date plate.
  • Hands that feel real. They cast real shadows on the dial.
  • An always-on view for when the screen rests.

Who FORGED is for

Wear something with grit. For anyone who loves the look of a real instrument but wants a smartwatch underneath it. If you would rather your watch looked like equipment than another digital screen, FORGED is for you.

Questions about FORGED

Is the second hand a smooth sweep?

Yes. The second hand sweeps smoothly instead of ticking, and the hands cast real shadows on the dial.

Can I try FORGED first?

Yes. FORGED has a two-day trial listing on the Garmin Connect IQ store, so you can wear it for two days before deciding.
