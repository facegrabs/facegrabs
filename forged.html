FORGED

The watch your friends will notice before they even say hi.

A watch that says something about you

Most watch faces look like a screen. FORGED looks like a tool that has been used, and it says something about you before you say a word.

Some things look better after they have been through a little hell. FORGED is built on that idea: weathered steel, rust and scratches, lit by a warm orange glow, so your wrist carries something that looks like it earned its place. Built to look earned.

Every scratch earned

  • Deep mechanical detail. Weathered steel, rust and scratches under a warm orange glow.
  • A mountain valley behind the dial, with a small hidden detail in the terrain for you to find.
  • Three sub-dials for steps, heart rate and battery, plus a date plate.
  • Hands that feel real. They cast real shadows, and the second hand sweeps smoothly instead of ticking.
  • An always-on view for when the screen rests.

Who FORGED is for

Wear something with grit. For anyone who loves the look of a real instrument but wants a smartwatch underneath it. If you would rather your watch looked like equipment than another digital screen, FORGED is for you.

Questions about FORGED

What is FORGED?

FORGED is an analog watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. It looks like a tool that has been used: weathered steel, rust and scratches under a warm orange glow, with deep mechanical detail.

What does FORGED show?

Hour, minute and second hands, three sub-dials for steps, heart rate and battery, and a date plate, set against a mountain valley with a small hidden detail in the terrain.

Is the second hand a smooth sweep?

Yes. The second hand sweeps smoothly instead of ticking, and the hands cast real shadows on the dial.

Does FORGED have an always-on view?

Yes, FORGED has an always-on view.

Which Garmin watches does FORGED work on?

FORGED works on round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches, including the fenix 7, 8 and 9 families and fenix E, epix 2, many Forerunner models, Venu 3 and 4, vivoactive 5 and 6, Enduro 3 and 4, MARQ 2 and 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Approach S70 and Instinct 3 AMOLED.

Can I try FORGED first?

Yes. FORGED has a two-day trial listing on the Garmin Connect IQ store, so you can wear it for two days before deciding.
