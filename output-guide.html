Output setup guide

Pick your five numbers, then learn to read the score.

What is Output and how do I install it?

Output is a Garmin watch face that turns five numbers you choose into one score: how much you have available today.

You pick the five metrics, how much each one counts and what color each one is. The face then shows the score and what is holding it back, so you know when to push and when to rest.

How do I install Output?

Open the Output page on the Connect IQ store on your phone, or search for Output in the Garmin Connect IQ app, and install it. It reaches your watch the next time the watch syncs. If it does not show up as your watch face, pick it from the watch face list on your watch. Want to wear it first? The trial listing runs the full face for two days.

Where are the Output settings?

Output keeps its settings in the Garmin Connect IQ phone app. Open Connect IQ, go to My Device or Watch Faces, choose Output, then Settings. The face updates as soon as the new settings reach the watch.

Output has no settings menu on the watch itself.

What does each Output setting do?

There are five metric slots. Each slot has the same three settings, so there are 15 settings in all.

Metric 1 to Metric 5
What that slot measures. Eight choices: Resting heart rate, VO2 max, Demand (training load), Active minutes, Steps, Floors, Body Battery or Stress.
Metric 1 importance to Metric 5 importance
How much that metric counts in the score: Low (x1), Normal (x2) or High (x3). A High metric counts three times as much as a Low one.
Metric 1 color to Metric 5 color
The color of that metric on the arc and in its pill: Pink, Violet, Orange, Sky blue, Green, Yellow, Red, Teal, Blue or White.

What are the default Output settings?

  • Metric 1: Resting heart rate, High, Pink
  • Metric 2: VO2 max, Normal, Violet
  • Metric 3: Demand (training load), High, Orange
  • Metric 4: Active minutes, Low, Sky blue
  • Metric 5: Steps, Low, Green

What does each Output metric measure?

Every metric is scored from 0 to 100 against your own recent normal, not against anyone else. The short name is the label you see on the face.

Resting heart rate (RHR)
Today's resting heart rate against your normal over the last four weeks. Lower than normal scores higher.
VO2 max (VO2)
Your VO2 max against its four-week average. Rising scores higher.
Demand, training load (DMND)
Each workout counts as how hard its sport is, times its minutes. Output compares your last 7 days with your last 28. About even is ideal. Far above means you are carrying fatigue. Far below means you are losing fitness. Output learns how hard each sport is for you from your heart rate during workouts.
Active minutes (MIN), Steps (STEP), Floors (FLR)
Your 7-day average against your normal.
Body Battery (BATT)
Your Body Battery reading right now.
Stress (STRS)
Today's average stress, turned around so that less stress scores higher.

Output does not use sleep, food or hydration.

What happens if I pick the same metric twice?

Only the first one counts. The repeat slot is not scored and shows two dashes.

How do I read the Output face?

  • The arc across the top is your available output. Each metric adds its own share in its own color, so you can see what the score is made of.
  • The big percent is the score: the weighted average of your five metrics.
  • FITNESS TREND has an arrow. Green and up means you are getting fitter. Yellow and flat means you are holding. Red and down means you are losing fitness. It compares your last four weeks of training with the four weeks ending a week ago.
  • The time and the date sit in the middle.
  • The five pills at the bottom are your five metrics. Each fills to its score, in its color, with its short name under it.
  • The underlined name in color marks the metric that is holding you back the most.

How do I open the detail view on Output?

Touch and hold the watch face. The detail view stays up for about ten seconds. It shows:

  • Your score, as a percent AVAILABLE, and the metric that is HOLDING YOU BACK.
  • Each metric with its importance (x1, x2 or x3), a bar for its score and a line with the real numbers, such as your resting heart rate today and your normal.
  • Your last workout: the sport, how long, when, and its Demand points.
  • Seven pills, one per day: yesterday, today, then five days ahead. The days ahead assume no new workouts.
  • Your trend in words: FITNESS RISING, FITNESS LEVEL or FITNESS FALLING.

What does the dimmed screen show?

On AMOLED watches the always-on screen shows the time in grey and a thin arc for your score. It shifts a few pixels each minute to protect the screen.

Why is Output not doing what I expect?

Why does a pill or the score show two dashes?

There is no data for that metric yet, or it repeats a metric in an earlier slot. The detail view says why, with a line such as NO RESTING HR, NO VO2 MAX YET, NO DATA or NO READING.

Why does Output say LEARNING YOU?

The trend needs some history first. The arrow appears once you have a little training on record, or a VO2 max with a few days behind it.

Why does Demand say NOT ENOUGH WORKOUTS YET?

Demand is built from your recorded workouts. Record a few and it starts to score.

Why does a metric say NOT ON THIS WATCH?

Body Battery and Stress need a watch that provides those readings. Pick a different metric for that slot.

Why does it say HEART RATE UNRELIABLE: USING DEFAULTS?

Heart rate failed Output's quality check on more than half of your recent workouts. Until it reads well again, Output uses a standard hardness for each sport instead of your own.

Why did the score not change right away?

Output works the score out again every 15 minutes, and straight away when you change a setting.

Why does the face only show the time and "trial ended"?

That is the trial listing after its two days. Install the full Output face from the Connect IQ store to carry on.

Common questions

How do I change the metrics on Output?

Open the Garmin Connect IQ app on your phone, go to your watch, open Output and choose Settings. Pick a metric, an importance and a color for each of the five slots.

What does the Output score mean?

It is how much you have available today, as a percent. Each of your five metrics is scored from 0 to 100 against your own recent normal, and the score is their weighted average.

What do the short names under the pills mean?

RHR is resting heart rate, VO2 is VO2 max, DMND is Demand (training load), MIN is active minutes, STEP is steps, FLR is floors, BATT is Body Battery and STRS is stress.

Why is one name underlined on Output?

That metric is holding your score back the most today. Touch and hold the face to see the numbers behind it.

How do I see the details behind my Output score?

Touch and hold the watch face. The detail view shows each metric, your last workout and a seven-day view from yesterday to five days ahead.

Which watches does Output work on?

Round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches including fenix 7, 8 and 9, fenix E, epix 2, many Forerunner models, Venu 3 and 4, vivoactive 5 and 6, Enduro 3 and 4, MARQ 2 and 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Approach S70 and Instinct 3 AMOLED. The full list is on the Output page and the store page.

Can I try Output before I buy it?

Yes. The trial listing on the Connect IQ store runs the full face for two days. After that it shows only the time and a note that the trial ended. The full face is $2.99 on the Connect IQ store.
