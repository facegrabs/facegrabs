Output

Your training. Your numbers. Your rules.

Why a score you cannot question is not much use

Most readiness scores hand you a number and ask you to trust it. A 62 on a day you feel great. A 90 on a day you feel flat. No way to tell why.

Output starts from a different idea: you know your training better than a formula does. You pick the five numbers that matter to you, you decide how much each one counts, and the score is built from that. When it moves, you can see what moved it.

What Output does for you

  • One score, your way. Five metrics you choose and weight, instead of a mystery formula.
  • The reason, not just the number. It shows what is driving your score, so you know whether to push or hold back today.
  • It learns how hard each sport is for you, from your own heart-rate response, so a hard ride and an easy run are not treated the same.
  • A trend arrow you can read in a glance. Building, leveling off or slipping.
  • Touch and hold for the detail view, with the week behind your score.

Who Output is for

For runners, riders and anyone who trains with a plan and wants the watch to answer one question honestly: how ready am I today, and why? If you have ever ignored a readiness score because you could not tell where it came from, Output is for you.

Questions about Output

What is Output?

Output is a watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. It turns five numbers you choose into one readiness score and shows what is driving it, with a trend arrow that says whether your fitness is building, leveling off or slipping.

How is Output different from a standard readiness score?

A standard readiness score is a formula you cannot see into. With Output you choose the five metrics and how much each one counts, and the face shows what is holding your score back or lifting it, so the number always comes with a reason.

Does Output know the difference between a hard session and an easy one?

Yes. It learns how hard each sport is for you from your own heart-rate response, so a hard ride and an easy run are not treated the same.

Which Garmin watches does Output work on?

Output works on round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches, including the fenix 7, 8 and 9 families and fenix E, epix 2, many Forerunner models, Venu 3 and 4, vivoactive 5 and 6, Enduro 3 and 4, MARQ 2 and 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Approach S70 and Instinct 3 AMOLED.

Can I try Output before I decide?

Yes. Output has a two-day trial listing on the Garmin Connect IQ store, so you can wear it for two days before deciding whether to keep it.
