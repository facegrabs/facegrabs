Output

Know when to push. Know when to rest.

Your body. Your rules.

You define readiness. You pick your core metrics and how much each one counts, and Output tracks them for you, all day, in one score that is yours.

Most readiness scores hand you a number and ask you to trust it. A 62 on a day you feel great. A 90 on a day you feel flat. No way to tell why.

Output starts from a different idea: you know your body better than a formula does. You pick the five numbers that matter to you, you decide how much each one counts, and the score is built from that. When it moves, you can see what moved it, so you can trust it.

See what's holding you back

  • One score, your way. Five metrics you choose and weight, instead of a mystery formula.
  • The reason, not just the number. The big number says READY, and the face names what is holding it back, like LOW: RESTING HR, so you know whether to push or hold back today.
  • All workouts aren't equal and your tech should get that. It scores every workout from its own heart rate, so a hard ride counts for more than an easy run of the same length.
  • Watch yourself get stronger. The TRAINING arrow shows in a glance whether your training load is building, level or easing off.
  • Touch and hold for the detail view, with your score for each of the last 7 days.

Who Output is for

For runners, riders and anyone who trains with a plan and wants the watch to answer one question honestly: how ready am I today, and why? If you have ever ignored a readiness score because you could not tell where it came from, Output is for you.

Questions about Output

What is Output?

Output is a watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. It turns five numbers you choose into one readiness score and shows what is holding it back, with a TRAINING arrow that says whether your training load is building, level or easing off.

How is Output different from a standard readiness score?

A standard readiness score is a formula you cannot see into. With Output you choose the five metrics and how much each one counts, and the face shows what is holding your score back or lifting it, so the number always comes with a reason.

Does Output know the difference between a hard session and an easy one?

Yes. It scores every workout from its own heart rate, so a hard ride and an easy run of the same length are not treated the same.

Which Garmin watches does Output work on?

Output works on round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches, including the fenix 7, 8 and 9 families and fenix E, epix 2, many Forerunner models, Venu 3 and 4, vivoactive 5 and 6, Enduro 3 and 4, MARQ 2 and 3, D2 Mach 1 and 2, Approach S70 and Instinct 3 AMOLED.

Does Output cost anything?

Output is free on the Connect IQ Store, so you can install it and try it at no cost.
