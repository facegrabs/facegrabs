Output

Your training. Your numbers. Your rules.

The problem

Not everyone trains the same way, so the readiness score should not be the same for everyone.

What it does

  • One readiness score built from five metrics you choose and weight, instead of a mystery formula.
  • It shows what is driving the number.
  • It learns how hard each sport is for you from your own heart-rate response.
  • A fitness-trend arrow shows whether you are building, leveling off or slipping.
  • Touch and hold opens a detail view.
