50-word description

FORGED is an analog watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. It looks like a piece of equipment, not another digital screen: weathered steel, rust and scratches under a warm orange glow, with a mountain valley behind it. It works on fenix 7/8/9 and fenix E, epix 2, Forerunner 70/165/170/255/265/570/955/965/970, Venu 3/4, vivoactive 5/6, Enduro 3/4, MARQ 2/3, D2 Mach 1/2, Approach S70, Instinct 3 AMOLED.

150-word description

FORGED is an analog watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches, built to look like a piece of equipment instead of another digital screen. The dial is weathered steel with rust, scratches and deep mechanical detail under a warm orange glow, set against a mountain valley with a small hidden detail in the terrain. Three sub-dials show steps, heart rate and battery, and a date plate sits on the dial. The hands cast real shadows, and the second hand sweeps smoothly instead of ticking. It has an always-on view. Its line is “Built to look earned.”. It works on fenix 7/8/9 and fenix E, epix 2, Forerunner 70/165/170/255/265/570/955/965/970, Venu 3/4, vivoactive 5/6, Enduro 3/4, MARQ 2/3, D2 Mach 1/2, Approach S70, Instinct 3 AMOLED. It is available on the Garmin Connect IQ store. Every FaceGrabs face has its own character and is built around a single problem it is meant to solve.

Steady

Fitness without the guilt.

Product pageConnect IQ storeTwo-day trialYouTube Short

Images, click to download:

Hero image Wrist photo

50-word description

Steady is a digital watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. One tapered ring fills from steps, active minutes and active calories, scored as a rolling 7-day average, so a rest day does not read as a failure. It works on fenix 7/8/9 and fenix E, epix 2, Forerunner 70/165/170/255/265/570/955/965/970, Venu 3/4, vivoactive 5/6, Enduro 3/4, MARQ 2/3, D2 Mach 1/2, Approach S70, Instinct 3 AMOLED.

150-word description

Steady is a digital watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches that measures your week, not your worst day. One tapered ring fills from steps, active minutes and active calories together, scored as a rolling 7-day average. Yesterday still counts, a rest day does not read as a failure, and a single huge day cannot close the ring for a week. It adjusts for fitness level (VO2 max and resting heart rate) and for a draining or stressful day. You set your own calorie target and how full the ring has to get. Nothing leaves the watch. Its line is “Fitness without the guilt.”. It works on fenix 7/8/9 and fenix E, epix 2, Forerunner 70/165/170/255/265/570/955/965/970, Venu 3/4, vivoactive 5/6, Enduro 3/4, MARQ 2/3, D2 Mach 1/2, Approach S70, Instinct 3 AMOLED. It is available on the Garmin Connect IQ store. Every FaceGrabs face has its own character and is built around a single problem it is meant to solve.

Output is a digital watch face for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches, made for runners and other athletes who do not train like everyone else. It shows one readiness score built from five metrics you choose and weight, instead of a mystery formula, and it shows what is driving the number. It learns how hard each sport is for you from your own heart-rate response, and a fitness-trend arrow shows whether you are building, leveling off or slipping. Touch and hold opens a detail view.
