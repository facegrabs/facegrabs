About FaceGrabs

FaceGrabs is an independent studio that makes watch faces for round AMOLED and color-screen Garmin watches. We believe a watch face is the thing you look at most, so it should be your biggest fan, not a critic. We are all on the same journey, and every face meets you where you are: Steady closes your ring on a rolling 7-day average, Output builds a readiness score from the metrics you choose, FORGED is a rugged analog face that looks earned, Slab is big, bold time in your colors, and Pea-k Performance is a free little green Activity Buddy who grows with your week. We build from what wearers ask for.

You do not have to use this wording. Facts and compatibility are on this page and on each store listing, so they are easy to check. All images here are available for editorial use.

Pea-k Performance

Keep him happy, keep him healthy, keep him active.

Pea-k Performance is a free watch face for round AMOLED Garmin watches, built around a little green pea named Pea-k. He starts in a pea pod and grows through eleven forms, from pod all the way to Pea-st Mode, as your last 7 days of steps and active minutes add up. He is funny, he sleeps and dreams, and he never nags. It is in review with Garmin and coming soon, so it is not on the Connect IQ Store yet.

Steady

Your journey isn't a straight line.

Steady is a free digital watch face that measures your week, not your worst day. One ring fills from steps, active minutes and active calories together, scored as a rolling 7-day average, so a rest day does not read as a failure and one great day can still close it. It adjusts for fitness level and for a draining day, and a Level setting (Beginner, Intermediate or Expert) sets how much it takes to close the ring.

Output

Know when to push. Know when to rest.

Output is a free digital watch face for runners and other athletes. It shows one readiness score built from five metrics you choose and weight, instead of a mystery formula, and it names what is holding the score back. It scores every workout from its own heart rate, and a TRAINING arrow shows whether your training load is building, level or easing off.

FORGED

The watch your friends will notice before they even say hi.

FORGED is an analog watch face built to look like a piece of equipment instead of another digital screen: weathered steel, rust and scratches under a warm orange glow, set against a mountain valley with a small hidden detail in the terrain. Three sub-dials show steps, heart rate and battery, the hands cast real shadows and the second hand sweeps smoothly. $1.99, with a two-day trial listing.

Slab

Your watch, your brand.

Slab is a digital watch face with huge, clean time you can read at a glance, set up your way: five finishes (steel, ice, carbon, bronze and frosted glass), 29 accent color sets, a separate color for each tile and its text, five time fonts, any two of 14 data fields and a bottom gauge in three styles. $1.99, with a two-day trial listing.

Steady Widget and Output Readiness

For people who keep their own face.

Steady Widget and Output Readiness are Garmin widgets for people who like the watch face they already wear. Steady Widget puts the Steady 7-day ring and your 7-day average in the glance loop, with streaks, your bests and badges. Output Readiness shows one number and one word for how ready you are today, scored from five metrics against your own normal. Both work on their own, without changing your watch face.
