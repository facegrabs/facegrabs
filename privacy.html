Privacy policy

Short and plain. Last updated November 27, 2026.

The short version

Our watch faces and widgets do their work on your watch. They send nothing to FaceGrabs unless you set up Squad Goals, Duel or Race Day in Pea-k Performance, which store the items listed in those sections below. This page explains the small amount of information our website, message board and forms keep. Questions? Email hello@facegrabs.com.

Our watch faces and widgets

FaceGrabs faces and widgets, including Pea-k Performance for Garmin and for Wear OS, read your steps and activity on the watch to draw what you see. Other than Squad Goals, Duel and Race Day below, the watch faces do not send this information anywhere. We do not use accounts, ads or trackers in the watch faces.

Squad Goals in Pea-k Performance (optional)

Squad Goals is off until you type a squad code or squad name in the Pea-k settings on your phone. Until then the face does not contact FaceGrabs servers. This section covers Pea-k Performance 1.4.1 and later. Earlier versions call it the group climb and send a smaller set of the items below.

What is stored on FaceGrabs servers, only if you set up Squad Goals

  • A random player ID and a private token the watch makes for itself. The server keeps a one-way hash of the token, not the token. No name, email or account is stored.
  • Your squad: its name, its share code, and that you are a member.
  • Once a day: an effort score from 0 to 100, your movement as a percent of your own usual day, your intensity minutes, and a yes or no for a high-effort day.
  • Only if you choose them: a handle (3 to 16 letters or digits you type), a random squad nickname if you join Heating Up, lift requests, and improvement board numbers.
  • Improvement board (opt in): how much your resting heart rate has dropped and how much your VO2 max has risen, each as a percent against your own first week. The board ranks these percents inside your squad, with ties sharing a rank, and shows the top few next to a random nickname, plus your own line to you.
  • Your network address is turned into a salted one-way code that is used to limit abuse and is not stored with your squad data.

Not sent: heart rate samples, step counts, location, sleep and workouts.

Purpose: to add up the squad's effort, show the squad page, award high-fives, lifts and kudos, and rank the improvement board if you opted in.

Where: on FaceGrabs' own server, a Cloudflare Worker with a Cloudflare D1 database. Cloudflare keeps ordinary server logs.

How long it is kept

  • When you leave a squad, your handle, nickname, daily detail, lift requests and board numbers are deleted right away. Your anonymous daily scores are removed after about a week.
  • If you do not use Squad Goals for 90 days, everything tied to your player ID is deleted automatically.
  • A squad with no members for 90 days is deleted.

Leaving and deleting

To leave a squad, clear the squad code in the Pea-k settings on your phone. To delete your data now, turn on "Delete my server data" in the Pea-k settings (called "Delete my squad data" before version 1.5). The watch asks the server to delete everything for your player ID and switches the setting back off. This needs your phone connection. If you cannot do that, email hello@facegrabs.com with your squad name.

Your progress page

The Your progress page on the watch compares your resting heart rate, VO2 max, daily steps and active minutes with your own first week. These numbers are calculated on the watch, and the page needs no squad, phone or internet. "Restart my progress" in the settings starts that baseline again.

Pea-k is for fun and motivation. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor any condition, and its numbers are not medical advice.

Duel and Race Day in Pea-k Performance (optional, version 1.5 and later)

Both are off until you turn them on in the Pea-k settings in Garmin Connect. They use the same random player ID and token described under Squad Goals, and the same server.

Duel: what is stored

  • The duel code, its length, when it starts and ends, and that you are in it.
  • Your handle, or a random squad name if you have no handle.
  • Your own best: two numbers worked out on your watch from your last 7 days (a movement number and an intensity number).
  • One duel score per day of the duel: how your day compared with your own best, from 0 to 150.
  • The duel standings the server works out from those scores, to send back to the watches in the duel.

The people in your duel see your handle or random name, your duel score, and a "resting" mark if your watch has not checked in for 3 days. They do not see your own best or your daily scores.

Race Day: what is stored

  • Your race date, sport, distance and goal time (if you set one), and whether the race is paused.
  • For this week and last week: your session minutes for that sport, your longest session, and whether a session was at your goal pace. If the watch has no recorded sessions for that sport, your intensity minutes are used instead and the server notes that.
  • Your usual weekly minutes and longest session, used to set the starting point of the plan.
  • Whether a session on race day covered most of the distance, and whether it beat your goal time.
  • The plan and the short summary the server works out and sends back for the watch to show.

Nobody else sees your race.

Purpose: to run the duel and its standings, and to work out your race plan. Where: the same Cloudflare Worker and D1 database as Squad Goals.

How long it is kept

  • Duel rows are deleted 30 days after the duel ends. Leaving a duel (clearing the code) deletes your days in that duel.
  • Race rows are deleted 30 days after the race date, or as soon as you turn Race Day off.
  • If your watch does not check in for 90 days, everything the server holds for your player ID is deleted.

To delete it now, turn on "Delete my server data" in the Pea-k settings. It deletes your Squad Goals, Duel and Race Day data from the server, leaves your squad and duel, and turns Race Day off. Duel and Race Day use your phone's connection to Garmin Connect, about every 30 minutes, the same way Squad Goals does.

Connecting your Garmin account (optional, if we offer it)

Some FaceGrabs features may let you choose to connect your Garmin account so we can use extra health summaries, such as sleep and heart rate variability, that a watch face cannot read by itself. This is off unless you connect. You connect through Garmin's own sign-in and consent screen, and we never see your Garmin password.

What we receive and why: only the summaries you agree to share, used only to work out the numbers shown to you on your own watch. We do not sell this data, share it with advertisers, or combine it with other people's data to compare or rank you.

How long we keep it: only as long as you stay connected, and no longer than needed to work out your numbers. History we store is limited to the days those numbers need.

Your choices: you can disconnect at any time in your Garmin account settings or by emailing hello@facegrabs.com. When you disconnect or ask us to delete your data, we remove what we stored about you within 30 days.

Where it is kept: on a small service we run with Cloudflare, keyed to a random ID, not your name or email.

This website

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages are read. Google Analytics uses cookies to tell visits apart and does not identify individual visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like most web hosts, GitHub and Cloudflare keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

The message board

When you post, we store your message, the nickname you choose (or a random one), and an optional stage label. We also store a scrambled, one-way code made from your connection details, which rotates weekly, so we can stop spam and repeat posts. It cannot be turned back into your IP address. Please do not post emails, phone numbers or other personal details. You can ask us to remove a post by emailing us.

The suggestion form

If you send an idea, we keep the idea, the face you picked, and your email only if you choose to give one so we can reply. Ideas are read privately and are not shown on the site.

Beta tester sign-ups

If you join the FaceGrabs Beta Lab, we keep your email, your watch model and any optional details you add (a first name or nickname, what you do, country and language, and your wish list). We use them only to run the Beta Lab: to send you test builds and ask for feedback. We never sell or share them. If a test needs a Google account email, we use it only to add you to that test. To leave and have your details deleted, email hello@facegrabs.com.

Polls

Poll votes are anonymous. Each vote is stored with a rotating one-way code, which changes every week and cannot be turned back into who you are. We use it only to stop duplicate votes and to let you change your answer. A vote holds no name, email, IP address or other personal data.

On the polls page you can also, if you want, give your email to hear about new FaceGrabs faces and updates. The box is never ticked for you. We keep your email only if you tick it and send it. It is used only for FaceGrabs news, it is never sold or shared, and it is kept apart from your vote, so we cannot tell what you voted for. To unsubscribe at any time, email hello@facegrabs.com and we will remove you.

Email

If you write to hello@facegrabs.com, we use your message and address only to reply.

What we never do

We do not sell your information. We do not run ads. We do not build a profile of you. We do not share your information with third parties except the services that run our site (GitHub, Cloudflare and Google for email and forms), which handle it under their own policies.

Children

Our faces are made for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing hello@facegrabs.com. Tell us the nickname or email involved and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced on our message board.
