Privacy policy

Short and plain. Last updated November 21, 2026.

The short version

Our watch faces and widgets do their work on your watch. They collect nothing about you unless you choose to join a group climb in Pea-k Performance, which shares one small daily number. This page explains the small amount of information our website, message board and forms keep. Questions? Email hello@facegrabs.com.

Our watch faces and widgets

FaceGrabs faces and widgets, including Pea-k Performance for Garmin and for Wear OS, read your steps and activity on the watch to draw what you see. That information stays on the watch. We do not collect it, send it anywhere, or share it. We do not use accounts, ads or trackers in the watch faces. The one exception is the group climb below, and only if you turn it on.

Group climb in Pea-k Performance (optional)

Pea-k Performance has an optional group climb. It is off until you type a climb code or a group name in the face's settings. If you never do, the face never contacts us.

If you join, your watch sends through your phone: a random ID made for the climb (not your name, email or Garmin account), the group code or group name you typed, and one effort number from 0 to 100 for each day. The number is worked out on the watch from your own activity compared with your own usual. Your steps, heart rate, location and other health details never leave the watch.

People in your group see the group's total progress. They do not see your daily number, and there are no rankings of effort.

Inside a group you can also turn on the improvement board, which is off until you choose it. If you do, your watch also sends three small numbers: how much your resting heart rate has dropped, your VO2 max has risen and your fitness age has fallen, each as a change from your own starting point (for example "down 3%"), never the readings themselves. Your group sees these changes next to your random climb name, not your real name. Turn the board off in the face's settings at any time and your numbers are removed.

Cloudflare, which runs the service, keeps ordinary server logs. To have your climb entries deleted, email hello@facegrabs.com with your group code.

This website

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages are read. It does not use cookies and does not track individual visitors. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and like most web hosts, GitHub and Cloudflare keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

The message board

When you post, we store your message, the nickname you choose (or a random one), and an optional stage label. We also store a scrambled, one-way code made from your connection details, which rotates weekly, so we can stop spam and repeat posts. It cannot be turned back into your IP address. Please do not post emails, phone numbers or other personal details. You can ask us to remove a post by emailing us.

The suggestion form

If you send an idea, we keep the idea, the face you picked, and your email only if you choose to give one so we can reply. Ideas are read privately and are not shown on the site.

Beta tester sign-ups

If you join the FaceGrabs Beta Lab, we keep your email, your watch model and any optional details you add (a first name or nickname, what you do, country and language, and your wish list). We use them only to run the Beta Lab: to send you test builds and ask for feedback. We never sell or share them. If a test needs a Google account email, we use it only to add you to that test. To leave and have your details deleted, email hello@facegrabs.com.

Email

If you write to hello@facegrabs.com, we use your message and address only to reply.

What we never do

We do not sell your information. We do not run ads. We do not build a profile of you. We do not share your information with third parties except the services that run our site (GitHub, Cloudflare and Google for email and forms), which handle it under their own policies.

Children

Our faces are made for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete anything we hold about you by emailing hello@facegrabs.com. Tell us the nickname or email involved and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. Big changes will be announced on our message board.
