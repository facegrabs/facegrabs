Slab Pro

Big digital time, your way.

The problem

A clean, big digital time that reads at a glance, set up exactly the way the wearer likes it.

What it does

  • Huge clean time, readable at a glance.
  • Five finishes: steel, ice, carbon, bronze and frosted glass. Steel has a brushed-metal texture.
  • 27 accent color sets, solid colors and two-tone pairs.
  • A separate color for each tile and for its text, so you can build any look, including your own flag or team colors. Matching tiles shade themselves so the layers stay clear.
  • Five time fonts.
  • Any two of 14 data fields: steps, temperature, heart rate, resting heart rate, Body Battery, stress, sunrise/sunset, altitude, floors, calories, distance, active minutes, recovery time, battery days.
  • A bottom gauge (battery, step goal, Body Battery or weekly active minutes) in three styles.
  • Seconds as digits or an edge ring.
  • The always-on screen keeps the same layout.
