Slab

Your watch, your brand.

Colors to match your vibe, your outfit, or your night, around huge, clean time you can read at a glance.

Build your story

Your watch is on your wrist all day. It should look like you: colors to match your vibe, your outfit, or your night.

Slab starts with huge, clean digital time you can read mid-stride, then hands you the controls: finishes, colors for every tile and its text, fonts and the numbers you care about. It does not have to look like anyone else's, and it still tells you the time without a squint. That is the point.

Make it distinctly yours

  • Huge clean time, readable at a glance.
  • Over a billion color combinations. Five finishes, 14 backgrounds, 37 accent sets, three accent shades and 20 colors for each of the time card and the three tiles multiply to 1,243,200,000, before text colors, fonts and data fields.
  • Five finishes: steel, ice, carbon, bronze and frosted glass. Steel has a brushed-metal texture.
  • 37 accent color sets, solid colors, two-tone pairs, Rainbow, Khaki + Olive and eight newer themes that also color the dial and tiles.
  • Military and natural tile colors: Olive Drab, Coyote Tan, Ranger Green, Khaki and a Camo set. They look best on a light background such as Ice or Silver.
  • Battery colors: color the battery gauge green, yellow or red by level.
  • Tile colors that let the metal show. Each color goes on as a wash over the finish, so the steel, carbon or bronze grain shows through, and the time card sits raised above the other tiles.
  • A separate color for each tile and for its text, so you can build any look, including your own flag or team colors.
  • Five time fonts and seconds as digits or an edge ring.
  • Any two of 14 data fields: steps, temperature, heart rate, resting heart rate, Body Battery, stress, sunrise/sunset, altitude, floors, calories, distance, active minutes, recovery time, battery days.
  • A bottom gauge (battery, step goal, Body Battery or weekly active minutes) in three styles.
  • An always-on screen that keeps the same layout.

Who Slab is for

For anyone who wants a big, readable time and wants to make the watch their own: runners who glance mid-run, people who like to match a kit or a team, and anyone who has ever wished a face came in their colors.

Questions about Slab

Can I set a different color for each tile?

Yes. The top left tile, top right tile, time card and bottom tile each have their own color and their own text color. Matching tiles shade themselves a little apart so the layers stay clear, so you can build your own team colors or flag colors.

Where do I change the settings?

In the Garmin Connect IQ phone app, and on many watches from the watch face itself. The setup guide walks through every setting and shows looks you can copy.

Can I try Slab first?

Yes. Slab has a two-day trial listing on the Garmin Connect IQ store, so you can wear it for two days before deciding.
